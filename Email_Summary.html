21 Oct 2026 · SCC Times

Overview

SEBI amended Regulation 62-A of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. This change simplifies the mandatory listing requirement for non-convertible debt securities, restricting it to debt securities issued after a listed entity's existing debt securities have already been listed. This regulatory rationalization aims to streamline compliance for entities with established debt market presence.

India Impact  This amendment directly affects Indian listed entities by reducing the compliance burden for subsequent issuances of non-convertible debt securities once their initial debt securities are listed, potentially encouraging debt market participation.
