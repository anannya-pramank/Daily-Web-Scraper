European Commission Adopts Finalized Corporate Sustainability Reporting Standards - ESG Today

06 Jul 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

ESG Today reported the European Commission adopted finalized Corporate Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS), integral to the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD). These standards mandate comprehensive disclosure of environmental, social, and governance impacts and risks for a broad range of companies operating in or with significant ties to the EU, with staggered implementation from fiscal year 2024. The ESRS specify detailed reporting requirements across various sustainability themes.

India Impact  Indian firms with significant operations, subsidiaries, or substantial revenue streams from the EU will be directly impacted by the CSRD and these finalized ESRS, requiring them to comply with extensive new disclosure obligations for their European activities.
