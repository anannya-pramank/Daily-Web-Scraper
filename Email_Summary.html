EU Greenwashing Rules Take Effect With Fines Exceeding 4% of Turnover

16 Oct 2026 · ESG News

Overview

New EU greenwashing rules have taken effect, introducing stricter consumer protection regulations that expose businesses to substantial financial penalties for misleading environmental claims, ESG News reports. Fines for non-compliance can exceed 4% of a company's annual turnover, emphasizing stringent enforcement. These rules, part of the EU's Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition Directive, aim to ensure greater transparency and accuracy in environmental marketing, prohibiting generic claims without substantiated, verifiable proof and enhancing consumer trust.

India Impact  Indian firms exporting to the EU, or multinational firms with EU operations, must urgently review their environmental marketing and disclosure practices to ensure full compliance with these stringent new rules, as violations could lead to significant financial penalties.
