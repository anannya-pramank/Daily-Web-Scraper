Meta, Rockefeller and Sylvera Back Brazil Carbon Data Project

30 Jul 2026 · ESG News

Overview

Sylvera has launched an open-data initiative designed to strengthen carbon market investment within Brazil's Atlantic Forest. The project, backed by Meta and the Rockefeller Foundation, will generate high-resolution forest carbon data. This data is intended to enhance transparency and reliability within carbon markets, providing more robust information for investors and participants. The initiative targets improved measurement and verification of carbon credits in a key global biodiversity hotspot.

India Impact  As India develops its own carbon credit market, such initiatives in other emerging economies could serve as benchmarks for enhancing data integrity, transparency, and investor confidence, potentially influencing domestic methodologies for forest carbon projects.
