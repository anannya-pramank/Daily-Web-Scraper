Pushing One Health approach, U.P. priorities early identification and reporting of emerging zoonotic diseases

10 Aug 2026 · The Hindu

Overview

Uttar Pradesh is prioritizing the early identification and reporting of emerging zoonotic diseases as part of a "One Health" approach. Mukesh Kumar Meshram, Additional Chief Secretary for Animal Husbandry, Dairy Development, and Fisheries Department, confirmed this initiative. The Animal Husbandry Department is collaborating with the Forest and Wildlife Department under the RISE Global Health Security (RISE GHS) Project to enhance health security measures within the state. This represents a proactive stance on public and animal health.

India Impact  This state-level policy in Uttar Pradesh signals increasing government focus on health security and biodiversity, potentially influencing operational requirements or supply chain due diligence for firms in agriculture, food processing, or pharmaceuticals with operations or sourcing in the region.
