Overview

Mondaq reports on the new UK Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), designed to ensure carbon costs are applied to imports from countries with less stringent carbon pricing. This mechanism aims to create a level playing field for UK industries in carbon-intensive sectors, requiring importers to report embedded emissions.
