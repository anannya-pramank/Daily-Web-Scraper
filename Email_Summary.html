Groundwater
Visakhapatnam’s groundwater falls 3.86 metres in a year; six villages past 20 metres deep

09 Sep 2026 · The Hindu

Overview

Groundwater levels in Visakhapatnam have fallen by 3.86 metres in one year, with six villages now exceeding a 20-metre depth. This decline is attributed by the District Ground Water Officer to a 47.89% rainfall deficit, extensive borewell extraction, and a lack of recharge caused by concrete surfaces. A total of 135 habitations, including 39 urban wards, are experiencing significant water table depletion.

India Impact  This situation in Visakhapatnam highlights the critical water stress challenges faced by Indian urban and rural areas due to climate variability and resource mismanagement, impacting water security and potentially future industrial operations.
