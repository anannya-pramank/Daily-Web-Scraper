ESG Rating Providers Face FCA Authorisation From June 2027 Under New Conflict-of-Interest Rules

03 Nov 2026 · ESG News

Overview

The UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) will introduce a new regulatory framework requiring ESG rating providers to obtain FCA authorization starting June 2027. This marks the first time such entities will be subject to direct regulation, primarily focusing on managing conflicts of interest and enhancing transparency in ESG data provision. The regime aims to improve the reliability and integrity of ESG ratings, which are increasingly crucial for investment decisions, by ensuring that providers adhere to stringent operational and conduct standards.

India Impact  This UK regulatory move sets a precedent for oversight of ESG rating providers, potentially influencing how Indian regulators like SEBI might consider future frameworks for domestic or international ESG rating agencies operating in India, impacting their methodology and governance.
