Canada Proposes New Oil and Gas Production Decarbonization Category for Sustainable Finance Taxonomy

09 Jul 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

Canada's Business Future Pathways (BFP) initiative has released a draft proposal for a new 'Production Decarbonization Category' within its sustainable finance taxonomy. This category aims to classify oil and gas operations that are actively engaged in significant decarbonization efforts. The proposal, reported by ESG Today, is part of broader efforts to guide investment towards climate-aligned activities, specifically targeting heavy-emitting sectors for transition finance.

India Impact  This development in Canada's taxonomy provides a model for how India might classify transitional activities within its own emerging sustainable finance framework, particularly for domestic energy and industrial sectors aiming for decarbonization.

Quinbrook Secures $780 Million For UK Renewable Energy Fund

10 Jul 2026 · ESG News

Overview

Quinbrook Infrastructure Partners has successfully closed its Renewables Impact Fund II (QRIF II) at £587 million (approximately $780 million), exceeding its initial target of £500 million. The oversubscribed fund, highlighted by ESG News, will focus on investing in UK renewable energy projects. QRIF II aims not only for renewable generation but also targets improvements in grid stability and enhanced biodiversity alongside its energy investments.

India Impact  While specific to the UK, this substantial fund close indicates continued strong investor appetite for renewable energy infrastructure, which could positively signal opportunities for similar large-scale capital deployment into India's growing renewable sector.

EthiFinance, ESG Book Merge to Form European Sustainability and Credit Rating “Champion”

09 Jul 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

EthiFinance, a European sustainable finance ratings and research provider, and ESG Book, a sustainability data and technology company, are merging. This strategic consolidation aims to create a dominant European entity in sustainability and credit ratings, described as a 'champion' by ESG Today. The combined entity will offer a comprehensive suite of sustainability data, research, and ratings services to cater to the growing demand in the European market.

India Impact  The formation of a larger European ESG ratings firm highlights global consolidation trends in the sustainability data market, which could influence how ESG data providers service Indian markets or how Indian firms access global ratings.

TenneT Germany Raises €3.5 Billion in Largest-Ever Corporate EuGB-Aligned Green Bond Offering

08 Jul 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

TenneT Germany, the country's largest electricity transmission system operator, has successfully raised €3.5 billion through its latest green bond offering. This issuance, reported by ESG Today, represents the largest-ever corporate green bond aligned with the European Green Bond (EuGB) standard. The funds are earmarked for financing eligible green projects aimed at expanding and modernizing Germany's electricity transmission infrastructure to support renewable energy integration.

India Impact  This record-setting EuGB-aligned issuance demonstrates the increasing scale and sophistication of green finance instruments globally, providing a benchmark for large Indian infrastructure entities considering similar green bond offerings to fund their sustainability transitions.

What a dolphin’s death in Kerala reveals about India’s marine plastic pollution

10 Jul 2026 · The Hindu

Overview

An endangered Indian Ocean humpback dolphin found deceased on Kozhikode beach in Kerala, India, had ingested plastic fishing net, leading to its death. This incident, reported by The Hindu, highlights the escalating threat of marine plastic pollution, specifically 'ghost nets,' in Indian waters. Data from the Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute (CMFRI) further indicates a rise in marine mammal strandings across the region.

India Impact  This incident underscores the growing environmental risks and potential for future regulatory scrutiny on plastic waste management and fishing practices for Indian firms operating in coastal regions or relying on marine resources.
