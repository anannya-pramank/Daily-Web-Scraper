Overview

Garanti BBVA introduced a treasury solution designed for companies exporting to the European Union. This derivatives-based product aims to help manage carbon price risk associated with the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM). The launch provides a financial tool for businesses to hedge against potential carbon costs, mitigating financial uncertainty stemming from EU climate regulations. This targets firms needing to navigate the evolving compliance landscape under CBAM and its financial implications for trade.

India Impact  Indian exporters to the EU will find such derivatives-based solutions increasingly relevant for managing financial risks related to CBAM liabilities, signaling an emerging market for hedging tools that could support their compliance efforts.

Carbon Market
EU Plans Emissions Charges for Waste Incinerators

24 Jul 2026 · ESG News

Overview

The European Union intends to extend its flagship carbon market, the EU Emissions Trading System (ETS), to waste incineration plants. This plan will require these facilities to pay for their carbon dioxide emissions, integrating a politically sensitive public service into the bloc’s carbon pricing scheme. The European Commission is driving this initiative, aiming to further incentivize emissions reductions across more sectors and advance the EU's climate targets by expanding the scope of its carbon market to a new industry.

India Impact  This expansion of the EU ETS highlights a global trend towards broader carbon pricing, signaling that Indian firms involved in waste management or considering waste-to-energy projects may face similar regulatory pressures or market expectations in the future.
