30 Jul 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

Greenhouse Gas (GHG) reporting framework provider GHG Protocol released results indicating strong opposition from a vast majority of companies to its proposals for tougher Scope 2 reporting requirements. India Impact  Given that many large Indian and multinational corporations active in India use or are expected to align with global GHG reporting standards for their BRSR filings, widespread corporate opposition to stricter Scope 2 rules could influence the pace or direction of future global and potentially Indian emissions reporting mandates.
