NORDEN, Microsoft Launch Maritime Book & Claim Pilot

04 Sep 2026 · ESG News

Overview

NORDEN and Microsoft, in collaboration with the Roundtable on Sustainable Biomaterials (RSB), launched a maritime Book & Claim pilot program. This initiative successfully delivered the first verified maritime emissions reductions through RSB’s registry, extending an already established system. The pilot aims to develop an innovative shipping decarbonization model, enabling companies to claim emissions reductions by supporting sustainable fuels even without direct cargo transport on green vessels.

India Impact  As a major maritime trading nation, Indian shipping companies and exporters could benefit from or be impacted by the evolution of such Book & Claim systems, facilitating decarbonization of their supply chains and enabling compliance with future emissions standards.
