'It's difficult to comprehend EU's unwillingness to embrace ISSB interoperability'

10 Oct 2026 · Environmental Finance

Overview

Environmental Finance highlights difficulties comprehending the European Union's reluctance to fully embrace interoperability with the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) framework. This creates reporting complexities for multinational corporations, necessitating distinct disclosure efforts for EU-specific requirements alongside ISSB-aligned standards. The lack of unified global reporting standards places an increased administrative burden on companies operating across diverse regulatory landscapes, impacting their ability to streamline sustainability data collection and disclosure processes.

India Impact  For Indian multinational corporations or those exporting to the EU, this lack of interoperability between ISSB and EU standards (like CSRD/ESRS) implies a higher reporting burden, potentially requiring parallel disclosure efforts rather than a unified approach, contrasting with India's BRSR Core which aims for alignment with global frameworks.

Amazon, Microsoft Fund German Wetland Project to Restore 750M Litres of Water

09 Oct 2026 · ESG News

Overview

Amazon and Microsoft are jointly funding a significant wetland restoration project in Hesse, Germany, with the objective of restoring 750 million litres of water, ESG News reports. This initiative targets a critical drinking water source for Frankfurt and is being implemented in partnership with the environmental organization NABU. The project focuses on groundwater replenishment through nature-based solutions, demonstrating substantial corporate investment in regional water security and ecological restoration by major technology firms.

India Impact  While specific to Germany, this initiative by multinational technology giants like Amazon and Microsoft signals a growing corporate trend towards investing in nature-based solutions for water stewardship, which could influence similar corporate social responsibility and environmental investment strategies among large Indian corporations operating globally or focusing on local water security challenges.
