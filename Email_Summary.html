Overview

The Council of the European Union has agreed its position on strengthening the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), specifically backing its expansion to finished goods. This agreement sets the stage for negotiations with other EU bodies to finalize the enhanced carbon import tax. The move tightens Brussels' carbon border rules, extending the mechanism's scope beyond primary goods to a wider range of imported products.
