Groundwater that glows yellow-green in U.P.

17 Aug 2026 · The Hindu

Overview

Decades of industrial waste dumping have severely contaminated groundwater with highly toxic chromium (VI) across parts of three districts in Uttar Pradesh, India. This widespread pollution exposes local villagers to unsafe drinking water and serious health risks, including potential carcinogenic effects. The issue highlights significant environmental degradation and the persistent challenges of managing industrial byproducts, posing a critical public health crisis and remediation challenge for the region.

India Impact  This incident underscores significant environmental and social risks for Indian industrial firms, highlighting potential liabilities from historical pollution, the importance of robust waste management, and the need for stricter environmental compliance to mitigate community health impacts.
