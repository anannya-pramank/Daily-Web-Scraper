New EU Anti-Greenwashing Rules Enter into Force

28 Sep 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

The EU's new Directive on Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition (ECGT) has officially entered into application, establishing stringent anti-greenwashing rules. This directive prohibits misleading environmental claims and requires companies to substantiate green claims with verifiable evidence. It aims to protect consumers from deceptive practices and promote genuinely sustainable products and services across the European Union market.

India Impact  Indian exporters and multinational firms with supply chains extending into the EU will need to rigorously review their environmental claims and marketing materials for products sold in the EU to ensure compliance with these new anti-greenwashing rules, potentially requiring enhanced due diligence and disclosure.

Green Bond
ESB Raises $575 Million in Ireland’s First European Green Bond Deal

25 Sep 2026 · ESG News

Overview

ESB, an Irish corporate issuer, has successfully raised approximately $575 million through its inaugural European Green Bond, marking Ireland's first use of the EU’s regulated green bond format. This 10-year bond issuance adheres to the EU Green Bond Standard, which sets strict requirements for the use of proceeds, external verification, and reporting. The deal signifies a growing commitment among European corporates to align sustainable finance with robust EU regulatory frameworks.

India Impact  Indian issuers contemplating green bonds, particularly those seeking to attract European capital, can observe this precedent-setting issuance under the EU's regulated Green Bond Standard to understand the stringent disclosure and reporting requirements that may become a benchmark for global sustainable finance.
