Punjab is reversing decades-long depletion of State’s groundwater table, says FM Cheema

07 Aug 2026 · The Hindu

Overview

Punjab Finance Minister Harpal Singh Cheema announced the state is reversing its decades-long groundwater depletion. To bolster groundwater recharge, municipal committees across various cities have mandated the inclusion of rainwater recharge wells for the approval of any new building site plans. This policy aims to ensure sustainable water management practices are integrated into urban development projects within Punjab, directly impacting construction and real estate sectors.

India Impact  This development directly impacts Indian firms, particularly those in the construction and real estate sectors operating in Punjab, by imposing a new mandatory environmental compliance requirement for project approvals.
