Paris Agreement
India and Japan Adopt Rules of Implementation for Joint Crediting Mechanism Under Article 6.2 of Paris Agreement - PIB

16 Jun 2026 · PIB India

Overview

India and Japan have adopted Rules of Implementation for the Joint Crediting Mechanism (JCM) under Article 6.2 of the Paris Agreement. This mechanism facilitates bilateral cooperation on carbon credit generation and transfer, aiming to promote decarbonization technologies and practices. The adoption of these rules establishes the framework for specific projects to be developed and implemented, allowing for the transfer of internationally traded mitigation outcomes between the two countries.

India Impact  This provides a structured bilateral pathway for Indian entities to generate carbon credits via emission reduction projects, enhancing access to carbon finance and technology transfer from Japan. It expands India's participation in international carbon markets beyond domestic frameworks.

Climate Risk
EBA Integrates Climate Risk into EU Banking Stress Test - ESG Today

16 Jun 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

The European Banking Authority (EBA) has integrated climate risk into its EU-wide banking stress test methodology. This move will require banks operating within the European Union to assess and report their resilience to various climate-related scenarios, including physical risks and transition risks associated with the shift to a low-carbon economy. This integration aims to enhance the financial sector's understanding and management of climate-related exposures.

India Impact  This development sets a precedent for climate risk integration in financial supervision that could influence RBI's future approaches to stress testing and risk management for Indian banks, particularly those with international operations.

Iberdrola Raises $1.7 Billion Green Bond to Fund Grid Expansion and Renewable Energy Investments

17 Jun 2026 · ESG News

Overview

Iberdrola has successfully raised €1.5 billion ($1.7 billion) through a European senior green bond issuance. This debt financing is earmarked to fund the company's grid expansion and selected renewable energy investments, demonstrating strong demand for green infrastructure debt. The issuance allows Iberdrola to secure capital for sustainable projects, aligning its financing strategy with its decarbonization and infrastructure development objectives.

India Impact  This significant green bond issuance underscores continued strong investor appetite for sustainable finance, signaling a positive environment for Indian infrastructure and renewable energy firms seeking similar capital through green debt instruments.

Anthropic joins Google, Stripe and others in $900 million carbon removal initiative - Trellis Group (formerly GreenBiz)

17 Jun 2026 · GreenBiz / Trellis

Overview

Anthropic has joined Google, Stripe, and other companies in a significant $900 million carbon removal initiative. This collaborative effort aims to accelerate the development and deployment of carbon removal technologies, signaling a growing commitment from major tech and industry players to address hard-to-abate emissions. The initiative pools capital to scale innovative solutions for capturing and permanently storing atmospheric carbon.

India Impact  This substantial private sector investment in carbon removal could inspire similar collaborative initiatives and technological advancements within India, potentially attracting foreign investment into India's emerging carbon capture and utilization sectors.
