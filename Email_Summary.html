A Compliance Market Nobody Enforces Is No Market at All: How Article 6 Is Killing the Supply It Was Meant to Create

ESG News · 24 Nov 2026 · Carbon Credits

Countries have authorized over 348 million carbon credits under Article 6, but only about 150,000 have been delivered, indicating a significant enforcement and supply issue. BlueGreen CEO Eyal Harel attributes this to sovereign red tape hindering compliance markets.

 

India impact  India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) could face similar challenges if enforcement mechanisms are not robust, potentially impacting its effectiveness in generating and trading credits.
