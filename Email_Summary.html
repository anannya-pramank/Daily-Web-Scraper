British Airways Signs SAF Purchase Deal Avoiding Nearly 200,000 Tonnes of Carbon Emissions

17 Jun 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

Biofuels producer EcoCeres announced a new multi-year transaction with British Airways for the purchase of sustainable aviation fuel (SAF). This significant agreement is projected to avoid nearly 200,000 tonnes of carbon emissions over its duration, contributing to the airline industry's decarbonization efforts. The deal underscores a growing trend in aviation to secure alternative fuels to meet sustainability targets and mitigate environmental impact, marking a substantial step for both companies.

India Impact  This transaction highlights the increasing global demand for SAF, which could spur investment in biofuel production capabilities within India or impact Indian airlines' sourcing strategies for similar emissions reduction targets.

Guest Commentary: SEC Rollback May Ease Rules, But Not Climate Risk

17 Jun 2026 · ESG News

Overview

Rajiv Jalim, Global Director of Sustainability Solutions at Novisto, provided a guest commentary to ESG News, asserting that the potential rollback of the U.S. SEC’s climate disclosure rules will not diminish the underlying momentum of corporate sustainability initiatives. His perspective suggests that despite regulatory easing, the inherent risks associated with climate change persist for companies, driving continued focus on environmental disclosures and practices regardless of specific mandates.

India Impact  While the SEC rollback is specific to the US, ongoing discussions about the stringency of disclosure requirements globally, including for BRSR Core, can draw parallels regarding the sustained pressure for corporate climate action independent of specific mandates.
