Carbon-aware scheduling reduces data center Scope 2 emissions

01 Oct 2026 · Economic Times

Overview

The Economic Times reports that "carbon-aware scheduling" is a strategy for data centers to reduce Scope 2 emissions. This method optimizes electricity consumption by scheduling energy-intensive tasks when the grid's carbon intensity is lower, leading to a measurable decrease in indirect greenhouse gas emissions associated with purchased electricity. This approach leverages real-time grid data to enhance operational sustainability and environmental impact.

India Impact  Indian corporations operating large data centers can implement carbon-aware scheduling to reduce their Scope 2 emissions, contributing to their BRSR reporting and overall decarbonization targets. This aligns with increasing pressure for ESG performance and energy efficiency within India's growing digital infrastructure sector.

ESG Today: Week in Review

04 Oct 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

ESG Today reports a significant shift by the UK's Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), transitioning its approach to sustainability and climate disclosures from a mandatory requirement to a "comply-or-explain" model. This change affects entities under FCA's purview, providing flexibility in meeting reporting obligations while still demanding transparency regarding adherence or deviation from established guidelines.

India Impact  This shift by the UK FCA to a "comply-or-explain" model for sustainability disclosures provides a point of comparison for SEBI and RBI, which have largely adopted mandatory frameworks like BRSR Core. Indian regulators might observe this approach for potential future refinements, balancing stringent requirements with industry adaptability.
