ESG Today: Week in Review

Overview

ESG Today reports that the EU Commission has officially adopted the finalized Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) along with its accompanying voluntary sustainability reporting standards. This adoption represents a critical regulatory milestone, establishing mandatory sustainability disclosure requirements for a broad spectrum of companies operating within the EU, including large non-EU undertakings with significant EU presence. The standards aim to significantly enhance transparency and comparability of corporate ESG performance across the bloc.

India Impact  Indian firms exporting to or operating within the EU, or those with significant EU subsidiaries, will be directly impacted by the CSRD, potentially necessitating enhanced and harmonized sustainability reporting aligned with EU standards, going beyond current BRSR requirements.
