Overview

A critique from BlueGreen CEO Eyal Harel, reported by ESG News, contends that Article 6 carbon markets are failing due to a lack of enforcement and excessive sovereign red tape. While over 348 million carbon credits have been authorized under Article 6, only approximately 150,000 have been delivered. This disparity highlights a significant bottleneck in supply, particularly as the 2028 deadline for CORSIA (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) approaches, jeopardizing the intended function of compliance markets.

India Impact  The dysfunction in Article 6 carbon markets, marked by low credit delivery despite high authorization, directly impedes India's ability to leverage international carbon finance mechanisms and sell its surplus credits, potentially affecting its climate action funding and competitiveness.
