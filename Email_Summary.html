EU Parliament Backs Expanded CBAM and Decarbonisation Fund

16 Sep 2026 · ESG News

Overview

The European Parliament has backed a broader Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), pushing the EU carbon levy deeper into industrial supply chains while tightening rules designed to prevent circumvention. Lawmakers adopted Parliament’s negotiating position, indicating expanded scope and stricter enforcement for covered goods. This development will increase the regulatory burden and cost for non-EU companies exporting high-carbon intensity products to the European Union market.

India Impact  Indian exporters of CBAM-covered goods (e.g., iron, steel, cement) will face increased compliance and financial costs due to the expanded scope and tighter rules, necessitating robust carbon accounting and reporting.
