Greenwashing Risks Under New EU Green Claims Rules

Overview

New EU Green Claims Rules introduce significant greenwashing risks for companies operating or selling within the European Union. These regulations aim to combat misleading environmental claims by establishing stricter verification and communication standards for product and company sustainability assertions. The rules demand robust substantiation of green claims, potentially increasing compliance burdens and legal liabilities for entities failing to meet enhanced transparency and accuracy requirements.
