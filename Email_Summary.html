Guest Commentary: SEC Rollback May Ease Rules, But Not Climate Risk

17 Jun 2026 · ESG News

Overview

Rajiv Jalim, Global Director of Sustainability Solutions at Novisto, argued in an ESG News commentary that the U.S. SEC’s climate disclosure rule rollback will not impede corporate sustainability momentum. He asserts that while specific regulatory burdens may ease, companies still confront inherent climate risks. This perspective suggests market and stakeholder pressures will continue driving corporate action on sustainability, regardless of the SEC’s revised position on mandatory disclosures.

India Impact  While the SEC rollback pertains to U.S. companies, it underscores a global trend where regulatory stringency can fluctuate; however, Indian firms operating under SEBI's BRSR Core framework still face clear, detailed disclosure obligations, potentially putting Indian standards at a more consistent level with international best practices on disclosure.
