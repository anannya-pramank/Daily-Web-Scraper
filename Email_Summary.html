06 Nov 2026 · ESG News

Overview

Gold Standard has published its first biochar removal methodology, titled "Production and Application for the Removal of Carbon via Biochar (PARC)." This new framework establishes a stringent 200-year storage test for carbon credits generated from biochar. Additionally, it defines three distinct monitoring tracks to ensure accurate and verifiable carbon sequestration, enhancing the integrity and long-term climate impact of biochar projects in the voluntary carbon market.

India Impact  Gold Standard's new biochar methodology provides an important framework for carbon credit generation, potentially opening avenues for Indian agricultural and waste management companies to develop biochar projects, participate in carbon markets, and attract climate finance with defined integrity standards.
