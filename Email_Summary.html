EU Commission Releases Draft Sustainability Reporting Assurance Standards

12 Nov 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

The European Commission has initiated a consultation process on newly released draft sustainability reporting assurance standards. These standards aim to enhance the reliability and comparability of reported sustainability information for entities operating within the European Union. This move by the Commission signals further regulatory development in corporate ESG disclosure and accountability within the EU framework.

India Impact  The EU's draft assurance standards for sustainability reporting will likely impact Indian companies operating in or supplying to the EU, requiring them to align their disclosure practices and potentially obtain third-party assurance to meet stringent European requirements. This contrasts with India's BRSR Core which currently does not mandate external assurance.

This analysis underscores a significant deficit in global funding required to address climate change, with Western governments facing pressures on other fronts.

India Impact  The significant global climate finance shortfall reported by Brazil suggests potential challenges for developing nations, including India, in securing necessary funding for climate adaptation and mitigation projects, potentially impacting India's climate action timeline.

Project Amrit Sarovar: Major push to water security in Ladakh as Lt. Governor clears 97 new ponds

15 Nov 2026 · The Hindu

Overview

Ladakh's Lieutenant Governor has approved 97 new ponds under Project Amrit Sarovar, accelerating water conservation efforts during winter. This initiative aims to strengthen climate resilience, ensure reliable irrigation, and safeguard rural livelihoods in the high-altitude cold desert. The administration views the harsh winter season as an opportunity to push forward these critical water security measures.

India Impact  This initiative highlights a specific regional government's proactive steps in climate adaptation and water management, which could serve as a model for other ecologically sensitive regions in India facing similar climate challenges, influencing regional ESG strategies.
