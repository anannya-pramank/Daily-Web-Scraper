21 Oct 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

Canada is reconsidering the inclusion of "abatement" categories for oil and gas activities within its sustainable finance taxonomy. This review targets whether such activities genuinely align with sustainability goals, especially regarding emissions reduction and climate targets. The decision will influence investment flows into the Canadian energy sector and set a precedent for how other jurisdictions define sustainable investments in transitional industries, impacting criteria for green bonds and other sustainable financial products.

India Impact  India, which is also developing its own sustainable finance taxonomy, may observe Canada's approach to incorporating transitional sectors like oil and gas with abatement technologies. Can India turn retrofitment into the next mobility revolution?

21 Oct 2026 · The Hindu

Overview

India has the potential to transform vehicle retrofitment into a significant mobility revolution, offering a distributed model for EV adoption. Unlike new EV manufacturing, which favors centralization and scale, retrofitment supports local workshops, technicians, and entrepreneurship. With appropriate training, neighborhood mechanic shops can become certified retrofitters, creating local employment and fostering a decentralized approach to sustainable transport solutions, potentially absorbing existing automotive workforce into the green economy.

India Impact  This concept presents a significant opportunity for Indian automotive companies and ancillaries to pivot towards a decentralized, retrofit-based EV ecosystem, potentially stimulating local economies and aligning with national goals for sustainable mobility and skill development across India.
