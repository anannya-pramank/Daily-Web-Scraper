Carbon Border
BRICS opposes 'discriminatory' carbon border taxes

12 Sep 2026 · Economic Times

Overview

BRICS nations have collectively declared their opposition to carbon border taxes, labeling these measures as 'discriminatory'. This significant stance from the economic bloc, comprising major emerging economies like India, signals a united front against trade mechanisms such as the European Union's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM). The group's opposition underscores concerns regarding the potential adverse impacts on international trade flows and the economic development trajectories of its member countries.

India Impact  India is a prominent BRICS member and a significant exporter to the EU, making this opposition crucial for advocating against the financial burden of CBAM on Indian industries and maintaining competitive trade relations.

Google Signs its Largest-Ever Superpollutant Carbon Credit Deal

10 Sep 2026 · ESG Today

Overview

Google has announced its largest-ever carbon credit deal, specifically targeting superpollutants. The transaction involves securing credits from projects focused on reducing highly potent greenhouse gases, indicating a strategic shift towards addressing short-lived, high-impact climate forcing agents. This move signals a significant corporate commitment within the voluntary carbon market to achieve ambitious climate targets by offsetting emissions from its operations.

India Impact  This deal highlights the growing corporate interest in superpollutant carbon credits, which could influence the demand for and development of similar projects in India, particularly those focused on industrial emissions or refrigerants.
